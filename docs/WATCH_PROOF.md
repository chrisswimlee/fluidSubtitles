# Watch proof note

Hosted CI cannot caption a live tab. After Screen Recording is granted, quit and reopen FluidSubtitles, then fill this on your Mac. Do not invent the result.

## Setup

- macOS 26, Apple Silicon
- Theater mode: Watch
- Capture: **This Mac** (not Safari or Chrome)
- Voice Engine: Apple Speech Analyzer (enough to try) or the engine that hears the video language
- I speak / Show as: a Korean, English, or Thai pair

## Steps

1. Grant Screen Recording if needed, then quit and reopen FluidSubtitles.
2. Open Theater → Watch → This Mac.
3. Press **Check capture**.
4. Play one Korean, English, or Thai YouTube (or another non-DRM app).
5. Press **Listen**. Wait for the first caption.
